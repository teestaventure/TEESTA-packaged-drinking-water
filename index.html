Teesta River Water Dispute

An overview of the transboundary water sharing challenges between India and Bangladesh.

Background

The Teesta River originates in the Himalayas and flows through Sikkim and West Bengal in India before entering Bangladesh. It is a vital water source for agriculture and livelihoods in both regions.

Key Issues

  • Water Allocation: Needs during the lean dry season (December to May) often exceed supply.
  • Agricultural Impact: Millions of farmers rely on the river for irrigation on both sides of the border.
  • Hydropower & Dams: Upstream dams and barrages significantly affect downstream water flow.
